**Reference**

The [Tutorial](../tutorial/readme.md) is one path through chartMaker, taken in order. These
four are the opposite: **dip into them when you need them, in any order, and none of them
assumes the one before it.**

**This is not a complete reference to every concept in the program**, and it is not trying to
be. It covers the things somebody actually using chartMaker has to look up. The
[design documentation](../../docs/readme.md) is where the whole of it lives, written for
somebody who wants to know how and why rather than what to click.

- **[Sources and TSD Files](sources_tsd.md)** - what a source definition contains, how to
  write one, and how to find out whether it works before you rely on it.
- **[The Catalog](catalog.md)** - the tile services chartMaker ships knowing about, asking a
  service what it publishes today, and turning an entry into a source of your own.
- **[Preferences and Keys](preferences_keys.md)** - where chartMaker keeps things and how to
  move them, how gently it talks to somebody else's server, and the key store.
- **[Housekeeping](housekeeping.md)** - the tile cache, cleaning up what browsing and
  surveying left behind, and looking after your own material.
